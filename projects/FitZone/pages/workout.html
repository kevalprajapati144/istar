Train your way.

Explore workout ideas for different muscle groups and fitness goals. Begin with a warm-up, use controlled movements and choose a level that feels right for you.

01

Chest workout

Build upper-body strength with controlled pushing movements.

  • Push-ups — 3 × 8–12
  • Chest press — 3 × 10
  • Chest fly — 2 × 12
Strength
02

Leg workout

Focus on lower-body strength, balance and stability.

  • Bodyweight squats — 3 × 12
  • Reverse lunges — 3 × 10
  • Glute bridges — 3 × 12
Lower body
03

Core workout

Improve core control and support everyday movement.

  • Plank — 3 × 20–30 sec
  • Dead bug — 3 × 10
  • Crunches — 2 × 12
Core
04

Arm workout

Train biceps, triceps and shoulder stability.

  • Biceps curls — 3 × 10
  • Triceps extensions — 3 × 10
  • Shoulder press — 2 × 10
Upper body
05

Back workout

Strengthen the muscles that support posture and pulling.

  • Resistance rows — 3 × 12
  • Bird-dog — 3 × 10
  • Lat pulldown — 2 × 10
Strength
06

Cardio session

Raise your heart rate with manageable intervals.

  • Brisk walk — 5 min
  • Skipping — 5 × 1 min
  • Cool down — 5 min
Endurance
Training note: These are general examples, not a personal medical or training plan. Warm up first, prioritise correct form, rest between sets and stop if you feel pain. If you have an injury or health concern, seek professional guidance.
